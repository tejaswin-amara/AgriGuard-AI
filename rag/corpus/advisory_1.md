# Leaf Blight Management

Leaf blight is a common fungal disease. Early detection is crucial.
If leaf blight is detected, farmers should ensure adequate spacing between plants to improve air circulation.
Avoid overhead watering, as wet leaves promote fungal growth.
For severe infections, consult a local extension officer for appropriate fungicide application.

# Soil pH Management

Optimal soil pH for most crops is between 6.0 and 7.5.
When pH drops below 5.5 (highly acidic), essential nutrients like phosphorus become locked in the soil and unavailable to plants.
Applying agricultural lime can help raise soil pH.
When pH is too high (above 8.0), nutrients like iron and manganese become deficient.
Adding elemental sulfur or organic matter can help lower pH over time.
